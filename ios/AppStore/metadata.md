## Description
Learn to chant Sri Harikathamruthasara of Sri Jagannatha Dasaru, one padya at a time, even while your hands are busy.

LISTEN AND REPEAT
• All 33 sandhis, from the Tadipatri Gurukula text, chanted with Vāgdhenu
• Half padya or full padya, with as many repeats as you like and a "your turn" pause
• The word being sung lights up, and you can tap any word to hear it
• Big-text view for practising during housework

RECITE AND CHECK
• Recite to your iPhone or iPad and see which words were right
• Pronunciation is checked on your device, and your voice is never uploaded
• Hands-free mode listens, responds and moves on by itself

READ KANNADA
• Vowels, consonants and kagunita with sound
• Say it: speak letters and simple words and find out whether they were right
• Letter games

YOUR LANGUAGE, YOUR SCRIPT
• App in English, Tamil, Kannada, Hindi, Telugu and Sanskrit
• Verses in Kannada with Tamil, Devanagari, Telugu or Latin script beneath

STAY WITH IT
• Daily goals, streaks, badges and gentle reminders
• Optional sign-in keeps your progress in step across iPhone, iPad and Android

## App Review notes
Microphone: used only in Recite & check and Say it; speech is processed on the device.
To test without an account, choose Continue as guest, or skip sign-in entirely; everything except cross-device sync works.
Chant audio is downloaded per chapter from the banner on the Home or Listen screens. The speech model (122 MB) is downloaded from the banner in Recite & check.
The app has no in-app purchases and no advertising.
