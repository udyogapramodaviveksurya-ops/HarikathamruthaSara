Privacy Policy: HarikathamruthaSaaraa Abhyaasaa

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what the HarikathamruthaSaaraa Abhyaasaa app ("the app"), published by TechByte (TECHBYTE INDIA ENGINEERING PRIVATE LIMITED), does with your information. You can use the app without an account.

Your voice

The app uses the microphone only when you start Recite & check or Say it. Your speech is checked on your phone by a speech model stored on your phone. Recordings are not uploaded or shared.

Information stored only on your phone

Without an account, your progress, settings and downloaded audio stay on your phone.

If you sign in (optional)

We use Google Firebase to run accounts and sync. Depending on how you sign in, this includes your email address, phone number, your Google name and profile picture, or the name and email (or private relay email) you share through Sign in with Apple, along with an account ID. Your learning progress and app settings are stored with your account so they can be restored on another device.

Notifications

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send update and news notifications, which involves an app-instance identifier and a messaging token. You can control notifications in the app's Settings or your phone's settings.

Problem reports

If you choose to report a problem, the app sends the sandhi and padya number, the type of problem, any note you type, and the app version. Reports are stored privately in Google Firebase, are not linked to your account, and can be read only by the developer. Please do not include personal information in your note.

Downloads

Chant audio, text and the speech model are downloaded from Hugging Face. As with any internet download, that service receives your device's IP address.

What we do not do

The app contains no advertising and we do not sell your information.

Security

Information sent by the app over the internet is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Keeping and deleting your data

Account data is kept until you delete it. To delete your account and its data, open the app, tap the profile picture on Home, and choose Delete account. If you can no longer use the app, email udyogapramodaviveksurya@gmail.com from the address on your account, or with your account's phone number, and we will delete it. Removing the app deletes the information stored on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
